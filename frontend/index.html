Why 137?

The fine structure constant from triangular numbers.

The fine structure constant α governs the strength of electromagnetism. Its inverse, approximately 137, has mystified physicists for a century. Feynman called it "one of the greatest damn mysteries of physics."

In Debemus, the answer is startlingly simple. Take the 16 basis elements of Cl(1,3). Count all distinct unordered pairs — this is the triangular number T(16) = 16 × 15 / 2 = 120. But wait: each element also pairs with itself via the Clifford product, contributing the 16 diagonal terms. So the total coupling count is T(16) + 16 = 136. Add 1 for the scalar (the void, the identity element), and you get:

\alpha^{-1} = T(16) + 1 = \frac{16 \times 17}{2} + 1 = 136 + 1 = 137
